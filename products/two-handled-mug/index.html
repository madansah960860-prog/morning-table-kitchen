What it is

Two handles, one on each side, each with a three-finger opening rather than the two most mugs offer. Held with both hands the weight is shared and the mug does not tip, which is the entire point and a considerable one for a full mug of hot tea. Ten ounces is deliberate: a sixteen-ounce mug of tea weighs over a pound and a quarter and is a different proposition. The stoneware is thick enough to hold heat for a good while and the base is unglazed, so it grips a table rather than sliding. The rim is rolled slightly outward, which pours into the mouth more cleanly than a straight rim. Microwave and dishwasher safe. Available in the speckled cream glaze; each one varies a little, which is what a speckle is.
