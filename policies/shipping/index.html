Shipping policy

Last updated September 21, 2026

What it costs

Two services. One charge per order, whether the box holds a peeler or eleven things.

Standard drops to nothing once the goods total passes $59. No code to type. The cart does the arithmetic. Tax and the shipping line itself are left out of that total, so the figure measured is the tools alone.

Expedited is never discounted. It bills at $14.95 on a small order and the same on a large one. Clearing the free-shipping line and then picking the faster service means paying for the faster service.

What the cart shows is what the payment screen shows. Nothing is appended afterward. No handling charge. No fuel adjustment. No second bill a week later.

Addresses we reach

The 48 contiguous states and Washington, D.C. PO boxes by Standard shipping only. No Alaska, Hawaii, Puerto Rico, US territories, or APO/FPO addresses yet.

PO box in the address field? Checkout will offer Standard and nothing else. Nothing is broken. A mail carrier can put a parcel inside the box itself, while the quicker service is a road delivery wanting a street door it can knock on. Switch the order onto a street address and both services come back.

Anywhere off that map, we cannot help yet. Put in an address outside it and you get a call or an email the same day, with the full amount returned. The carton is never taped shut in the first place.

How long the box sits here

1–2 business days is what we take. Inside that window a picker walks the shelves, matches the order to the slip, and closes the carton. Saturday and Sunday do not count toward it, and neither do federal holidays. Order at nine on a Friday evening and the carton gets built on Monday.

There are two clocks. Packing is ours. Transit is the carrier's, and the table above measures only the second one. Add them together for the day a box lands on your counter.

No tool here is built to order, and none of them sits waiting on a supplier before it can be boxed. The window covers picking and taping. That is the whole reason it stays short.

Every tool takes either service

Twenty-two products, and not one of them is freight. The heaviest thing on the shelves is a skillet. Both services stay open on every product page and in every cart, and neither switches itself off because of what you put in the box.

A PO box is the only thing that ever removes the faster option, and that is an address matter rather than a size one.

Who carries it, and watching it move

USPS and UPS both collect here. Which one gets a given box depends on what it weighs, how big it is, and where it is going. Speed is your decision. The truck is not.

The tracking number goes out by email as soon as the label prints. Give the link a day before worrying about it, since first scans routinely lag behind the pickup. Three business days with no new event on that page means a box is stuck. Let us know. We will open a trace with the carrier instead of leaving you to keep reloading.

If a promised date slips

Miss a date we published and the six steps below run. Nobody has to complain first. These steps are federal law rather than a house rule: the Federal Trade Commission put them in the Mail, Internet, or Telephone Order Merchandise Rule. A date only goes up here in the first place when we believe it is hittable.

  1. The warning lands before the date does. Not the week after it has quietly slid past.
  2. A replacement date comes with it. A real one. If we genuinely have no date to give, the message will say so in those words rather than make one up.
  3. Backing out stays available throughout, at no cost. Answer the email. Or telephone (555) 010-0100. Or fill in the form on the contact page. Nobody is charged a cent for choosing any of the three.
  4. Say nothing and, up to 30 days, we read it as a yes. The margin is measured against the date you were first quoted. A slip of 30 days or under, with no reply from you, leaves the order live.
  5. Over 30 days, or no date at all, and the default flips. We cancel, and the refund leaves without anyone asking for it. From that moment it takes a word from you to keep the order running, rather than a word from you to stop it.
  6. Two slips in a row and staying quiet stops working. The second one wants you to actually say yes. No answer, and we refund the order and close it.

Cancel under that rule with a card payment and the refund clears inside one billing cycle. Goods and shipping, the whole figure. None of it is held back.

Parcels that vanish, and boxes that arrive damaged

The scan says delivered and the step is empty. Two minutes of looking first: side door, porch box, whoever else lives there. A driver will tuck a parcel somewhere dry, and a stop occasionally gets marked before the van actually arrives. Still nothing after two business days? Tell us. Filing the carrier claim is our job, and the replacement or the refund goes out straight away. Neither one waits for that claim to settle.

A tracking page that stopped updating. Once seven business days go by without a single new event on it, the parcel counts as lost and we act. Tell us which you want, the same tool again or the money, and that is what happens.

Chipped, bent, or cracked on arrival. Two pictures settle it: the tool, plus the outer carton where that is what the damage started on. Both inside 7 days of delivery. Neither the return postage nor the replacement costs you a cent. Steps are on the Returns and refunds page.

Legally the goods become yours at delivery, and so does the risk in them. Standard stuff, repeated in the Terms of service. Practically it means very little, because chasing a carrier is a job that stays on this side of the counter.

An address with a mistake in it

Check the address on your confirmation email the day it turns up. Correcting one costs nothing and takes under a minute, provided the label has not printed yet. (555) 010-0100, or write to support@morningtablekitchen.com.

A carrier that hands the parcel back over a digit in the wrong place, or an apartment number nobody wrote down, means a full refund on the tools. Not on the shipping. That trip was made and paid for. Give us the right address and the order ships again at the standard rate.

Sales tax

Sales tax is calculated at checkout based on the shipping address, where required.

Tax shows up in one place: the payment screen, before you press the button. Whatever that screen says is what the card gets billed. Nothing is appended once the order is in.

Ask us about any of this

(555) 010-0100 is answered by a person during Monday–Friday, 9 a.m.–5 p.m. Eastern Time (closed weekends and federal holidays). Prefer to write? Send it to support@morningtablekitchen.com and the reply comes within 1 business day. Neither route needs a password, and neither does starting a return.
