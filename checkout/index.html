Shipping policy in full

Sending something back

Nobody will ask you why. You have 30 days once it lands, and a handle thicker than your hand expected counts for as much here as a manufacturing fault would.

Our label is $6.95, taken off the refund. Use your own carrier instead if that is cheaper. Broken, faulty, or the wrong item costs you nothing by either route, and no restocking fee applies to anything at all.

One exception, and it is absolute: anything that has been used with food stays with you. A pan that has cooked, a mug that has been drunk from. Unused and still in its packaging, it comes back.

We issue the refund inside 5 business days of the box landing back here, though your bank may sit on it for another week. Nothing is charged for calling an order off before dispatch.

Returns policy in full

Paying, and reaching a person

Accepted: Visa, Mastercard, American Express, Discover, Apple Pay, Google Pay. The card is entered on Stripe's own pages and the number is never handled here. Nothing to register for, nothing to remember.

Whatever total Stripe puts in front of you at the end, tax included, is the amount taken. There is no later addition.
