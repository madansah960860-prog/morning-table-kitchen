Thank you — the order is in

The card cleared and we have the order. Nothing further is needed from you today. Your cart is now empty, which is why this page looks bare.

What happens next

  1. Watch for the receipt. It should land in a minute or two, carrying the order number and the line items. Quarter of an hour with nothing? Look in the spam folder first, then telephone us.
  2. We box the order within 1–2 business days. Weekends and federal holidays do not count.
  3. A second email carries the tracking number the day it ships, whichever of USPS or UPS takes it.
  4. Delivery then takes as long as the service you picked: the estimate starts the day it leaves us, not the day you ordered.

Changing or cancelling it

Until a box leaves the building we can stop it, and there is no charge for doing so — telephone or email, whichever is quicker for you. After that it has to complete the journey, and what happens next is a return rather than a cancellation.

Wrong address? Call straight away on (555) 010-0100. We can usually correct it before the label is printed, and not afterwards.

Returns and refunds — 30 days, and what the $6.95 label covers

Keep the confirmation email

It is the only record you need. There is no account to log into here, and there never will be, so nothing is stored behind a password for you to retrieve. Quote the order number and we can find anything.
